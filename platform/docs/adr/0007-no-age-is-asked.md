# ADR 0007: No age is asked

**Status:** accepted, 28 October 2026 (owner decision).

## Decision

The platform doesn't ask anyone their age. There is no age field and no age gate.

## Why

Privacy and simplicity. An age is personal data the platform would otherwise not hold, and a gate built on it adds a step for every learner.

## The known risk

Brazil's ECA Digital does not accept a self-declared age, and Portuguese is a launch language.

## What the design already does

- **It collects minimal data:** an account is an email and a display name.
- **There are no ads and no profiling.**
- **There is no marketing aimed at children.**
- **AI calls request zero data retention** from the provider (the AI cost guard's rules).

## What would reopen it

- Legal advice that says otherwise.
- A launch aimed at Brazil.
