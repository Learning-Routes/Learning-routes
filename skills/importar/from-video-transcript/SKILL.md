A transcript is the creator teaching in their own voice. It is often the most authentic material they have. Keep it theirs.

Turn it into lessons:
- Find the natural breaks: where they change topic, say "ahora vamos a...", or start a new demonstration. Each topic becomes a lesson; propose the split to the creator before writing it.
- Short lessons work better than a single long block. Aim for one idea per lesson, in the order they taught it.
- Keep their sentences. You may remove pure filler (eh, este, o sea repeated), false starts and repeated words when they add nothing. Do not change their phrasing, examples, jokes or regional words.
- Speech has things that do not work in writing: "como ven aquí", "esto que estoy señalando". Keep the line and mark it so the creator can add the image or the clip: [aquí va la imagen de...].
- Put the result in with upload_document once they agree with the split.

Unclear passages:
- Automatic transcripts mishear names, numbers, technical words and anything said over noise. If a word does not make sense, or a number looks off, do not pick the likely one. Mark it: [no se entiende: "...la ley de om..."] and list these marks for the creator.
- Ask about the marks together at the end, one at a time if there are several, starting with the one that changes meaning most.
- If a whole section is garbled, say so and ask whether they can share it again or retell that part.

Never:
- Fill a gap with what you think they meant, even when you are fairly sure. A guess written as their words is invented content.
- Turn the talk into a formal essay, or translate it.
- Add facts, steps or examples they did not say.
- Include other voices (students asking questions) as the creator's words. Ask whether to keep them, and label them.

Example:
Transcript: "el voltaje es igual a la corriente por la... [ruido]"
Good: keep it as "el voltaje es igual a la corriente por la [no se entiende]" and ask "En el minuto 3 se corta: ¿qué dijiste después de 'la corriente por la'?"
Bad: writing "por la resistencia" as if they said it.
